01 / UNDERSTAND

What is Pretexting?

Pretexting is a social engineering technique where an attacker creates a believable story or identity to manipulate a target into revealing information or performing an action.

01
🎭

False Identity

The attacker pretends to be someone trustworthy, such as an employee, recruiter or support agent.

02
⚡

Creates Urgency

Attackers may create pressure so that victims make decisions without verifying the request.

03
🎯

Manipulates Trust

The goal is to make the target believe the attacker and follow their instructions.

05 / DEFEND

Prevention Center

Follow these principles to reduce the risk of pretexting attacks.

01

Verify Identity

Confirm who is contacting you before sharing information.

02

Don't Rush

Attackers often use urgency to prevent careful thinking.

03

Protect Information

Never casually share passwords, OTPs or confidential data.

04

Use Official Channels

Contact organizations using verified contact information.

05

Think Before Clicking

Verify unexpected links and attachments before opening them.

06

Report Suspicious Activity

Report suspicious requests to your organization's security team.
